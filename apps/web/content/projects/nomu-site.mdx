## Why I made it

I wanted one personal space for publishing writing and showing work without splitting it across disconnected platforms. The site is intentionally editorial, minimal, and easy to keep evolving in Git.

<Figure src="/projects/nomu-site/cover.png" alt="Current Nomu personal site homepage" />

## How it is built

The web app is a Next.js workspace with file-based MDX content and Zod-validated metadata, so publishing stays versioned and malformed entries fail during builds. Music and games use an admin-managed Neon database because they need browser-based CRUD; everything else remains plain content files.

Motion has one owner per job: GSAP handles hero and scroll timelines, Motion handles component interaction, and Lenis handles desktop smooth scrolling. A companion Remotion studio reuses visual primitives for teasers, article covers, and music visualizers.

## Current progress

The publishing, projects, tools, search, comments, theme preferences, audio player, music library, games, sitemap, RSS, and SEO foundations are in place. It is still an active build: the design system and content structure grow with each new piece of work.
