MAI reorders existing YouTube or CSV playlists, or generates new fixed-size sets, using musical transition scoring instead of a simple shuffle.

<Figure src="/projects/mai/cover.png" alt="MAI playlist engine model preview" />

It combines harmonic compatibility, tempo matching, beat and phrase alignment, and mood bridges to make adjacent tracks feel intentional. A beam-search generator explores possible orders, then a bounded 2-opt pass refines the result.

The project also includes a training pipeline: it scrapes curated mixes, resolves tracklists, mines hard negatives, and trains transition and energy-arc models. When data is sparse, MAI keeps its transparent music heuristics in charge rather than over-trusting a weak model.

Recommended orders export back to YouTube or YouTube Music, so the result is usable straight away.
