## Why I made it

TradePilot is an early-stage trade-intelligence startup where I lead technical development as CTO and founding member. The work runs from backend architecture and data-processing workflows to product-facing web features — application logic, APIs, AI-assisted features, and database-backed product flows.

## How it is built

I translate business requirements into deployable web systems: database schemas, internal tools, order-style flows, and product surfaces, owning technical decisions across implementation, deployment, and architecture with the founding team.

## Current progress

Trade Pilot placed 2nd in a World Learning Algeria competition associated with AI Forge / Algerian Youth in Action, and the platform is under active development toward its first production release.
