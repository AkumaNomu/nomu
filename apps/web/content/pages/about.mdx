# I'm Abdelmalek.

Software developer and Artificial Intelligence student building web applications, systems tools, browser software, and applied AI projects. My work spans TypeScript, Python, Rust, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Linux, and machine learning — from client products and startup software to local tools, automation, and experimental AI systems.

## What I do

I lead technical development as CTO and founding member of Trade Pilot, an early-stage trade-intelligence platform, and I build web products for startups and small businesses as a freelance developer — e-commerce sites, database-backed apps, auth, admin tools, and production deployments. Alongside that I keep a steady practice of open-source systems work: capture and OCR utilities, playlist intelligence, voice-controlled browsing, and Linux tooling.

## Teaching

I coach, mentor, and write course material for the Algerian Olympiad in Informatics and the Algerian Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence — algorithms, complexity, cryptography, and AI problem solving — and I served as an observer with Team Algeria at the 2025 International Olympiad in AI in Beijing. I also mentor STEM events like MathTec's π-thon.

## Background

I'm studying toward a joint inter-university BSc in Artificial Intelligence (Pavia, Milan, Milano-Bicocca), after a year of Computer Science at USTHB and a year of Mathematics at NHSM in Algiers. I also have a background in motion design and multimedia, which I bring into interface work when a project calls for it.
